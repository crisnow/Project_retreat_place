# Furstanwald House & Gardens — Website Demo

This project is a visual and interactive demo of a possible website for Furstanwald House & Gardens. It showcases the proposed look and feel, page structure, and examples of how visitors might explore stays, check dates, and get in touch.

## What the demo includes

- A responsive landing page with sections for stays, residencies, the house and gardens, and visitor information.
- A sample availability calendar to demonstrate a possible booking flow.
- Example enquiry and guest-information chat interfaces.
- Illustrative photos and sample website copy.

## Demo limitations

This is a prototype, not a complete or live booking website. The calendar uses sample availability; it does not check real inventory or reserve dates. Enquiry forms do not send or store submissions, and the chat is a demonstration rather than a connected support service. Photos, location information, and other content are illustrative and may not represent the final property or offering.

## Future development

The complete website and its production features have not been implemented yet. Future work may include connecting the booking flow to live availability and reservations, sending enquiries to the right team, integrating a real guest-support service, and replacing demo imagery and copy with approved property details. These features will be developed and confirmed separately before launch.
